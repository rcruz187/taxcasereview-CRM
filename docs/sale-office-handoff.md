# RomyLabs signed-sale → office handoff

A signed RomyLabs sales agreement can launch `/crm-admin/provision` with the prospect and agreement IDs. The New Office form is prefilled from the sale. After provisioning, the signed agreement is permanently linked to the new office, billing seats/rates are copied to the tenant, the prospect remains Won with its office ID, and the agreement appears in the office agreement/Documents record.
